Great video needs great audio. Dreamy 1.2 introduces our new AI Audio Enhancer. Powered by our in-browser neural networks, you can instantly remove hums, hisses, and background noise from your clips. We've also added smart auto-ducking, so your background music automatically lowers when someone is speaking.
